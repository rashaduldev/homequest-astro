## How long does it take to buy or sell a property?
The time it takes to buy or sell a property depends on several factors, including market conditions, property type, and the buyer's/seller's readiness. On average, it takes 1 to 3 months from listing a property to closing a sale.

## What fees are involved in buying or selling a home?
The fees may include agent commissions, closing costs, inspection fees, and legal fees. Always check with your local regulations.

## How is the value of my home determined?
Home value is determined based on location, condition, market trends, comparable sales, and professional appraisals.

## Can I get assistance with financing a property purchase?
Yes, you can get help from banks, mortgage brokers, or financial advisors to find the best loan options for your purchase.

## What are the benefits of hiring a property management service?
Property management services handle tenant screening, rent collection, maintenance, and ensure your investment property is well-managed.
